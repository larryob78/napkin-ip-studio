# Active Build Plan

## Objective

Build complete Version 1 IP bibles for Baby Demon and The Last Boy on Earth in parallel, using matched development stages and separate canon.

## Simultaneous map

| Stage | Baby Demon | The Last Boy on Earth | Shared review |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Parenting truth and comic premise | Loneliness, legacy and hope | Emotional truth |
| 2 | Family, Satan and neighbourhood | Boy, alien crew and Earth | Character architecture |
| 3 | Hell rules and domestic world | Earth mystery and alien rules | World coherence |
| 4 | Ten-episode comic escalation | Ten-episode mystery escalation | Season architecture |
| 5 | Horror-comedy visual system | Cinematic science-fiction system | Art-direction distinction |
| 6 | Family-comedy brand | Hopeful adventure brand | Brand ownership |
| 7 | Two-minute comic test | Two-minute emotional test | Tencent readiness |

## Task packets

### BD-01 Core family
Create the parents, Baby Demon and the central family relationship.

### LB-01 Core expedition
Create the boy and the principal alien expedition members.

### BD-02 Season pressure
Define what threatens the family's ability to remain normal across Season One.

### LB-02 Season mystery
Define the central question, clues, reversals and final revelation.

### BD-03 Episodes 1–10
Build ten distinct parenting problems that escalate the mythology and family arc.

### LB-03 Episodes 1–10
Build ten discoveries that escalate the mystery and emotional relationships.

### BD-04 Art direction
Lock horror realism, domestic contrast, Baby's design and repeatable visual motifs.

### LB-04 Art direction
Lock alien design, reclaimed Earth, Golden Record symbolism and cinematography.

### BD-05 Brand bible
Position the property as an iconic, affectionate horror-comedy about parenting.

### LB-05 Brand bible
Position the property as a hopeful global science-fiction adventure about humanity.

### SHARED-01 Character council
Score all principal characters for depth, contradiction, memorability and story engine.

### SHARED-02 Season council
Check classical arcs, cause and effect, escalation, midpoint, crisis and finale.

### SHARED-03 Visual council
Ensure the two properties are unmistakably different and visually ownable.

### SHARED-04 Tencent council
Confirm plot, episodic detail, art direction, visuals and proof-of-concept readiness.

## Definition of done

Each project must contain:

- Locked premise and human truth
- Complete principal cast
- Relationship map
- World rules
- Season arc
- Ten complete episode treatments
- Brand bible
- Art direction bible
- Production bible
- Two-minute proof-of-concept brief
- Executive summary
- Copyright and confidentiality notice

No section is marked complete below an 8/10 review score.
