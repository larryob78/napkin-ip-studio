## Page approval gate

A page cannot move to `Approved` unless it:

- matches current canon
- communicates one primary idea
- contains no unsupported lore
- has a clear executive purpose
- can be skimmed in under 45 seconds
- has an approved visual role
- uses the same editorial voice as the rest of the book
