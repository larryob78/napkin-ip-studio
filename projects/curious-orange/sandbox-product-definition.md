# Sandbox Product Definition

## Product in one line

**Sandbox turns live training into personalised, hands-on learning that continues after the session ends.**

## What we are building

Sandbox is a learning platform where people can:

1. Join a live session.
2. Ask questions and get answers from approved material.
3. Return later and search everything from the session.
4. Practise what they learned in a safe environment.
5. Get help from an AI mentor.
6. Continue from where they left off.

## The problem

Most training is passive and fragmented. A webinar ends, the recording is forgotten, questions are lost, and people rarely get to practise what they learned.

Companies use separate tools for live video, documents, courses, chat, and exercises. Nothing connects the full learning journey or remembers the learner.

## The solution

Sandbox connects three parts:

### Live

People watch, interact, ask questions, and get AI-supported answers during the session.

### Learn

The session, questions, answers, and supporting material become a searchable knowledge base.

### Lab

People apply the learning through a guided task or simulation, supported by an AI mentor.

## The core loop

**Live session → captured knowledge → questions answered → practical task → learner progress saved → better future learning**

This loop is the core product.

## Best first MVP

Build one complete journey:

> A person joins a live session, asks a question, gets a grounded answer, returns later, finds the material, and completes one practical exercise with an AI mentor.

The first MVP should include:

- One live session
- One searchable knowledge base
- One AI mentor
- One practical exercise
- One learner profile
- One simple admin area

## What not to build first

Do not begin by building the full platform.

Avoid custom-building:

- Video streaming infrastructure
- A full breakout-room system
- Dozens of integrations
- A complete LMS
- Fully generative interfaces
- Complex multi-agent orchestration

Use existing tools for video, authentication, transcription, and model access. Build the valuable custom layer around knowledge, memory, personalisation, and practice.

## Immediate next step

Bring in the CTO to create:

1. A one-page product architecture map.
2. One complete user journey.
3. A strict 6–8 week MVP scope.

The architecture map should show:

**User → Live → Knowledge Brain → Learn → Lab → Learner Memory → Better Future Experiences**

It should also separate:

- What already exists
- What needs to be built
- What can be integrated
- What belongs in the MVP
- What comes later

## Product category

**Enterprise AI / Learning Technology**

More specifically:

**An AI training and knowledge platform for companies.**

## YC-style pitch

**Companies spend heavily on webinars and training, but most of that knowledge is forgotten when the session ends. Sandbox captures each live session, turns it into a searchable company brain, and gives every learner a personalised AI mentor and practical environment where they can apply what they learned.**

## Strategic focus

The most important decision now is choosing the first narrow customer and first repeatable use case. The technology architecture should be designed around that real use case, not around every possible future feature.
