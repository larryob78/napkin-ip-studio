# BABY DEMON
## Tencent Development Decision Sheet v0.1

**Purpose:** Convert the remaining open canon questions into a controlled development sprint without prematurely locking unsupported story facts.

## 1. Decisions required before scripting

### A. The family
Lock:
- parent names, ages and occupations
- Baby Demon's name and exact age
- the couple's relationship history and current pressure points
- why they accept responsibility for the baby
- whether they know his origin at the beginning

**Decision test:** Their love must feel credible before the mythology expands. The family cannot exist only to service supernatural jokes.

### B. The New York social world
Lock a compact recurring ensemble with clear comic functions:
- one neighbour who sees too much
- one childcare or playgroup figure who normalises the impossible
- one relative who increases pressure on the parents
- one babysitter whose relationship with the baby can evolve

**Decision test:** Each supporting character must create social pressure, expose a different parenting insecurity and remain useful across multiple episodes.

### C. The neighbourhood
Choose one specific New York neighbourhood and define:
- apartment size and layout
- street texture and daily routes
- local park, bodega, childcare space and subway access
- social mix and neighbour proximity
- how the neighbourhood changes when supernatural events escalate

**Decision test:** The setting must feel observed and reusable, not like a collection of postcard locations.

### D. Satan and Hell
Lock:
- Satan's personality and visual grammar
- why he has not raised the child himself
- what he genuinely wants from the baby
- what powers Hell has over the family
- what rules prevent Satan from simply taking the child
- the force or intelligence described as older than Satan

**Decision test:** Satan must be threatening, funny and emotionally relevant. His conflict with the parents should resemble a supernatural custody battle without becoming a sketch premise.

### E. Baby's powers
Define a limited rule set for:
- emotional triggers
- physical range
- escalation and recovery
- whether powers are instinctive or learned
- what protects the parents
- what the baby cannot yet do

**Decision test:** Every power must create a parenting problem, reveal character or advance mythology. Spectacle alone is not enough.

## 2. Recommended supporting ensemble framework

This is a structural recommendation, not locked canon.

### The neighbour
Practical, observant and impossible to shock. Creates pressure around noise, building rules and unexplained damage while gradually becoming protective of the family.

### The playgroup leader
Treats every supernatural event as a developmental phase. Creates comic denial and gives Mum a recurring source of judgement, advice and reluctant validation.

### The relative
Loves the parents but questions their competence. Forces the couple to perform normal family life while concealing increasingly impossible evidence.

### The babysitter
Initially fearless and transactional, later develops an unusual bond with the baby. Provides a younger outside perspective and can carry stories when the parents briefly leave the domestic centre.

## 3. Two-minute proof decisions

The proof of concept must lock:
- Baby Demon's face, age, horns, eyes and infant movement
- the black leather jacket's tailoring, weight and continuity
- parent casting and emotional chemistry
- one hero apartment interior and one exterior New York location
- the visual boundary between ordinary reality and infernal contamination
- the final quality threshold for holding, feeding, eye-lines, skin, cloth, props and floor contact

## 4. Tencent development sprint outputs

1. Named family and one-page relationship history
2. Locked neighbourhood and repeatable location map
3. Four-character supporting ensemble
4. Baby power rules and escalation chart
5. Satan and Hell mythology statement
6. Two-minute script and shot list
7. Character, apartment and jacket continuity packs
8. Production test budget, schedule, rights and approval pathway

## 5. Quality gates

Do not approve a story or character decision unless it:
- begins with a recognisable parenting truth
- creates comedy through character rather than cruelty
- keeps the family emotionally central
- makes New York more specific
- supports repeatable episodic conflict
- preserves the baby's innocence
- can be communicated visually through serious horror craft

## Weakest remaining section
The recurring New York ensemble and exact neighbourhood remain unresolved. Locking these will make the social comedy as specific and repeatable as the family and supernatural engines.
