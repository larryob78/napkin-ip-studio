# BABY DEMON
## Canon v0.1

**Format:** Premium episodic animated comedy  
**Season:** 10 episodes × approximately 15 minutes  
**Status:** Confidential Development Material

## Locked premise

An ordinary family is raising the son of Satan. Everything in their world looks normal, grounded and photoreal. The baby alone looks unmistakably demonic. Nobody treats his appearance as remarkable until his emerging powers begin calling forth the forces of Hell.

## Locked genre and tone

- Comedy first
- Horror is the visual hook
- Family relationships provide the heart
- The supernatural escalation must remain rooted in ordinary parenting problems
- The world reacts with comic denial, rationalisation or misplaced normality

## Human truth

Having a baby is difficult.

Raising a child is difficult.

Raising the son of Satan...

...is Hell.

Parenthood is rewarding, exhausting, frightening, absurd and rarely discussed with complete honesty. The series turns that truth into an affectionate supernatural exaggeration.

## Core comic engine

1. Begin with an ordinary parenting challenge.
2. Baby's demonic nature intensifies it.
3. The parents try to solve it using ordinary parenting logic.
4. Their solution creates a larger supernatural consequence.
5. The family relationship is tested and ultimately strengthened.

Examples:
- Teething produces fangs.
- A tantrum opens a portal.
- First words summon an ancient creature.
- Playgroup resembles a ritual.
- Bedtime becomes an exorcism in reverse.

## Visual canon

- Photoreal, recognisable contemporary world
- Naturalistic lighting and camera language
- Baby Demon looks genuinely frightening but behaves like a baby
- Horror-quality creature detail contrasted with domestic normality
- Avoid cartoon-goth clichés and generic Halloween design

## Story guardrails

- Baby is not evil in an adult moral sense.
- The parents' love must be credible.
- Do not make parenting itself the villain.
- Satan and Hell may expand the mythology, but family remains the centre.
- Comedy must come from character and situation, not cruelty toward a child.
- Every episode must contain an emotional parenting truth.

## Open development questions

- Names, ages and backgrounds of the parents
- Baby's name and exact age
- Why the family has him
- Whether they know his origin at the beginning
- Satan's role and personality
- Recurring neighbours and family members
- Season One antagonist or pressure
- Finale event and new status quo
