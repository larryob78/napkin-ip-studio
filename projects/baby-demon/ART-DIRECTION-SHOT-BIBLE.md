# BABY DEMON — Art Direction Shot Bible
## Tencent Development Companion v0.1

**Status:** Confidential Development Material  
**Purpose:** Convert the approved New York visual direction into a coherent, repeatable image suite for the pitch deck and two-minute proof of concept.

## Visual thesis
A completely ordinary New York family is raising an impossible child. The world is photographed with serious premium horror conviction, while the baby is the only element that looks visually out of sync. The comedy comes from that contrast, never from broad visual gags.

## Non-negotiable image standard
- The apartment, streets, parents, props and parenting mess must feel observed and real.
- Baby Demon must withstand full-screen close-ups without appearing synthetic, toy-like or mascot-like.
- The baby remains recognisably infant-like in proportion, movement, need and vulnerability.
- The tiny black leather biker jacket is a signature costume with real tailoring, grain, stitching, creasing and weight.
- Horror lighting and framing are played seriously.
- Supernatural colour appears only when the baby's emotion affects reality.
- No frame should advertise the use of AI.

## Hero image suite
### 1. The family portrait
**Frame:** Mum, Dad and Baby Demon around a cramped New York kitchen table or high chair.  
**Purpose:** Explain the entire premise in one believable image.  
**Rule:** The parents behave naturally; nobody poses at the baby's appearance.

### 2. The leather-jacket close-up
**Frame:** Full-screen portrait of Baby Demon in the jacket, lit by normal apartment window light.  
**Purpose:** Establish the iconic character asset and technical quality bar.  
**Detail test:** Skin pores, fine hair, wet eyes, tiny nails, horn growth, leather grain and jacket stitching.

### 3. The stroller
**Frame:** Baby Demon moving through real pedestrian density on a Manhattan or neighbourhood sidewalk.  
**Purpose:** Prove the impossible child belongs in a socially alive real city.  
**Rule:** Background New Yorkers react with practical indifference, impatience or mild curiosity.

### 4. The brownstone stoop
**Frame:** The family paused on a lived-in stoop after a difficult night.  
**Purpose:** Ground the show in intimate New York domestic life and affectionate exhaustion.  
**Lens language:** 35mm observational framing with natural street texture.

### 5. The milestone
**Frame:** An ordinary feeding, teething, bath or bedtime moment beginning to bend reality.  
**Purpose:** Capture the episodic engine.  
**Rule:** Start with a recognisable parenting truth before any supernatural escalation.

### 6. Playgroup
**Frame:** A normal local baby group where a nursery rhyme has acquired subtle ritual geometry.  
**Purpose:** Show social comedy, pressure and horror in one composition.  
**Rule:** Other babies and parents remain completely photoreal and unstylised.

### 7. New York at night
**Frame:** A parent pushing the stroller past a bodega, deli or subway entrance under wet street light.  
**Purpose:** Expand the visual world beyond the apartment while preserving intimacy.  
**Palette:** Sodium-vapour amber, winter blue, aged brick, practical neon and deep black.

### 8. Hell enters the room
**Frame:** Baby Demon in an ordinary nursery as a small portal, mirror intrusion or baby-monitor disturbance contaminates the natural light.  
**Purpose:** Introduce mythology without losing the domestic premise.  
**Rule:** Effects remain restrained, physically integrated and motivated by the baby's emotion.

## Character portrait suite
### Baby Demon
- Neutral front portrait with emotionally readable eyes
- Joyful expression that remains slightly frightening
- Exhausted or tearful infant close-up with subtle heat distortion
- Full-body standing or crawling test showing real infant balance
- Jacket on, jacket off and bath-time continuity studies
- Horn, claw, fang and eye-detail sheets across emotional states

### Mum
- Warm, quick and exhausted rather than polished
- Portrait in natural apartment light
- Action frame solving a supernatural problem with ordinary parenting logic
- Emotional close-up choosing the child over social approval

### Dad
- Dry, practical and visibly sleep-deprived
- Domestic DIY frame confronting impossible damage
- Feeding or holding shot proving body weight and contact
- Protective close-up when he openly claims the baby as his son

### Satan
- Elegant, ancient, charismatic and emotionally wounded
- No cartoon horns, theatrical cape or comedy-villain styling
- Introduced through controlled reflections, screens or darkness before the full reveal

## Environment families
1. Cramped New York apartment kitchen, nursery and living room
2. Brownstone stoop and residential sidewalk
3. Neighbourhood bodega or deli at night
4. Subway entrance, platform or carriage
5. City park and local playgroup space
6. Babysitter environment and first-birthday location

Each environment family must support wide context, medium family interaction and close tactile detail.

## Cinematography rules
- 35mm–50mm lenses for domestic and street observation
- 85mm for infant, parent and material close-ups
- Natural window light and practical household sources
- Controlled horror push-ins used sparingly
- Handheld or gently observational movement during family chaos
- No sitcom master-shot coverage or visual joke punctuation
- Infernal red-orange enters as contamination, not a permanent grade
- Camera stays near adult or infant physical reality rather than adopting exaggerated monster angles

## Continuity and quality gates
- Stable baby anatomy, horn size, eye colour, skin detail and age
- Jacket cut, hardware, wear, sleeves and closures remain identical
- Correct infant weight and contact during holding, feeding, bathing and stroller use
- Parent faces, hair, wardrobe and apartment geography remain consistent
- New York signage and geography must feel plausible without relying on tourist landmarks
- Hands, food, bottles, toys, cloth and floor contact receive manual review
- Avoid plastic skin, duplicated extras, false text and generic AI faces

## Two-minute proof image ladder
1. Quiet apartment morning
2. Parent debate over feeding or sleep
3. Baby reveal in leather jacket
4. Ordinary milestone begins
5. Practical parenting solution
6. Restrained supernatural escalation
7. New York exterior consequence
8. Intimate family gesture proving love

## Canon boundaries
The following remain intentionally unresolved and must not be visually overcommitted before approval:
- Parent and baby names
- Exact neighbourhood and apartment profile
- Parents' occupations and ages
- Final mechanics of the delivery mix-up
- Full rules and limits of the baby's powers
- Supporting New York ensemble
