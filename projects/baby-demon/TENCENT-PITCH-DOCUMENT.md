# BABY DEMON
## Tencent Pitch Document v0.1

**Format:** Premium episodic animated comedy  
**Season:** 10 episodes × approximately 15 minutes  
**Status:** Confidential Development Material

## One-line premise

An ordinary family is raising the son of Satan, and every everyday parenting problem risks unleashing Hell.

## Logline

When a loving family discovers that their terrifying-looking baby is the son of Satan, they try to raise him with ordinary parenting logic, only for teething, tantrums and bedtime to trigger increasingly supernatural consequences.

## Series overview

Baby Demon is a family comedy with a horror visual hook. The world is contemporary, recognisable and photoreal. The baby alone looks unmistakably demonic. His parents love him completely and attempt to handle every crisis as though it were a normal stage of childhood.

Each episode begins with an ordinary parenting challenge. The baby's demonic nature intensifies it, the parents apply familiar parenting logic, and their solution creates a larger supernatural consequence. A tantrum may open a portal. First words may summon an ancient creature. Bedtime may become an exorcism in reverse.

The comedy comes from denial, misplaced normality and the clash between domestic routine and genuine horror. The heart comes from the truth beneath the premise: parenting is exhausting, frightening, absurd and deeply rewarding.

## Emotional engine

The central emotional engine is unconditional love under impossible pressure.

The baby is not evil in an adult moral sense. He is a baby. His parents must distinguish between frightening supernatural behaviour and ordinary emotional need. Every episode tests their patience, trust and partnership, then returns to the fact that they are a family.

## World bible

### The ordinary world

The series takes place in a grounded contemporary setting. Homes, streets, childcare spaces and workplaces should feel completely believable. The normality of the environment makes the baby's demonic appearance and powers funnier and more unsettling.

### Baby Demon

The baby looks genuinely frightening but behaves like a baby. His powers emerge through developmental milestones, emotions and accidents rather than calculated evil.

### Hell and the supernatural

Hell may expand the mythology, but family remains the centre of the story. Supernatural events should grow directly from a parenting problem rather than becoming detached fantasy spectacle.

### Social response

People react to the baby's appearance and powers with comic denial, rationalisation or misplaced normality. The world should resist acknowledging the obvious until consequences become impossible to ignore.

### Locked world rules

- Comedy comes first.
- Horror is the visual hook.
- Every episode begins with an ordinary parenting truth.
- The parents use ordinary logic to solve supernatural problems.
- Their solution creates a larger consequence.
- The family relationship is tested and ultimately strengthened.
- Comedy must never depend on cruelty toward a child.

### Canon still to be locked

- Family location and social setting
- Why the family has the baby
- Whether they know his origin at the beginning
- The exact rules and limits of his powers
- Satan's role and personality
- The structure and culture of Hell
- How much the outside world knows

## Character bible

### Baby Demon

**Locked function:** A genuinely frightening-looking baby whose developmental milestones have supernatural effects.  
**Core truth:** He is not evil in an adult moral sense.  
**Comic power:** His ordinary needs create extraordinary consequences.

**Canon still to be locked:** Name, exact age, personality details, power rules, visual signature, emotional attachment patterns and season arc.

### Parent One

**Locked function:** One half of the parenting partnership, committed to treating the baby's needs as manageable and normal.  
**Potential dramatic pressure:** Control, denial, exhaustion or fear of being judged.

**Canon still to be locked:** Name, age, background, work, flaw, parenting philosophy, relationship history and visual identity.

### Parent Two

**Locked function:** The other half of the parenting partnership, bringing a contrasting response to the baby's supernatural development.  
**Potential dramatic pressure:** Overprotection, curiosity, ambition, family loyalty or fear of the baby's origin.

**Canon still to be locked:** Name, age, background, work, flaw, parenting philosophy, relationship history and visual identity.

### Satan

**Locked function:** The baby's origin point and a potential source of mythology, pressure and comedy.  
**Guardrail:** Satan cannot displace the family as the emotional centre.

**Canon still to be locked:** Personality, goals, relationship to the baby, relationship to the parents, visual design and season function.

### Recurring world

Neighbours, relatives, childcare professionals and other parents should reflect familiar social pressures around parenting while escalating comic denial.

**Canon still to be locked:** Supporting cast, recurring locations and season antagonist or pressure.

## Season One arc

Season One follows the family as the baby's powers emerge alongside ordinary developmental milestones. Early episodes focus on denial and containment. Mid-season, the consequences begin connecting, suggesting that the baby's arrival has drawn attention from forces beyond the family home.

The parents' relationship is placed under growing strain as they disagree about what the baby needs, what he is and whether they can keep him safe. By the finale, the family must make a defining choice that proves their commitment to the child while establishing a new status quo for Season Two.

**The exact origin story, season antagonist, finale event and new status quo remain to be locked.**

## Episode guide

The following structure preserves the locked comic engine without inventing final canon.

### Episode 1: The New Baby
The family attempts to settle into life with a terrifying-looking infant while rationalising the first signs that his needs have supernatural consequences.

### Episode 2: Teething
Teething produces fangs and turns a familiar parenting milestone into a domestic containment crisis.

### Episode 3: Sleep Training
A bedtime routine becomes an exorcism in reverse as the parents discover that darkness, lullabies and separation trigger powers they do not understand.

### Episode 4: First Words
The baby's first recognisable word accidentally summons an ancient creature, forcing the parents to interpret what he was actually trying to communicate.

### Episode 5: Playgroup
An ordinary playgroup begins to resemble a ritual, while every adult present insists there is a perfectly normal explanation.

### Episode 6: The Tantrum
A public tantrum opens a portal and exposes the growing limits of the parents' ability to hide what is happening.

### Episode 7: The Babysitter
The parents attempt their first night away. Their carefully chosen babysitter interprets the baby's behaviour through a completely different belief system.

### Episode 8: Family Visit
A relative arrives with strong opinions about parenting and becomes the first person close to the family who may understand what the baby is.

### Episode 9: Dad Is Calling
A message, visitor or supernatural disturbance reveals that the forces of Hell have begun paying attention.

### Episode 10: A Family Decision
The family faces an external claim on the baby and must decide what they are prepared to risk to remain together.

**Episode titles, supporting characters, mythology reveals and final beats remain development placeholders until missing canon is locked.**

## Visual and art direction

- Photoreal contemporary world
- Naturalistic lighting and camera language
- Horror-quality creature detail
- Domestic spaces framed with emotional realism
- The baby appears genuinely frightening, never cute-goth or Halloween-themed
- Comedy created through contrast, timing and performance
- Avoid cartoon-goth clichés, purple-and-black fantasy shorthand and generic occult imagery

The visual ambition is to shoot the world like a premium live-action family drama while treating the baby's demonic anatomy and supernatural events with credible horror craftsmanship.

## Cinematic reference direction

Use references that support:

- Grounded family performance
- Natural domestic cinematography
- Horror imagery treated seriously
- Comedy emerging from situation rather than visual parody
- Creature detail with emotional readability
- A recognisable world disrupted by the impossible

**Specific approved film, photography and design references still need to be gathered into the canonical deck from the user's existing visual materials.**

## AI production approach

Napkin Studio will combine current AI image, motion, voice and production tools with human-led writing, direction, design and editorial control. The key technical challenge is maintaining a highly consistent baby character whose frightening design still supports subtle emotional performance.

The workflow should include:

1. Canon-controlled writing and comic story development
2. Parent and baby character design
3. Facial and body consistency testing
4. Domestic environment development
5. Supernatural effect and creature studies
6. Voice, timing and performance exploration
7. Human editorial, comedy and quality review at every stage

## Two-minute motion-test plan

The proposed test should prove the central contrast between credible family life and genuine horror imagery.

### Suggested sequence

1. A quiet, naturalistic domestic morning.
2. The parents prepare breakfast while discussing an ordinary parenting concern.
3. The baby is revealed in full, frightening detail.
4. A small developmental milestone begins.
5. The parents use ordinary parenting advice.
6. Their solution triggers an escalating supernatural consequence.
7. The sequence resolves on an intimate family beat rather than a horror punchline.

### Test goals

- Prove Baby Demon's character consistency
- Demonstrate believable emotional performance
- Establish the photoreal domestic world
- Test horror-quality supernatural effects
- Validate comic timing and contrast
- Establish a repeatable production pipeline

## Production considerations

- The baby's design must support both horror detail and emotional readability.
- Character continuity is the highest technical priority.
- Parent performance and comic timing must remain naturalistic.
- Reusable domestic environments should be developed early.
- Supernatural escalation should be designed around episode-specific parenting problems.
- The season mythology must be locked before detailed scripting.
- Final schedule and budget depend on the agreed test scope and delivery standard.

## Franchise potential

The core engine can sustain multiple seasons because every stage of childhood creates new emotional and supernatural possibilities. The world can expand into childcare, school, extended family, neighbours, Hell's bureaucracy and the baby's evolving relationship with his origin.

Franchise extensions may include short-form parenting moments, character-led digital content, illustrated books for older audiences, collectibles, games and interactive experiences built around the contrast between family normality and supernatural chaos.
