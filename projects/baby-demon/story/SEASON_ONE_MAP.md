# BABY DEMON — Season One Map

## Format
10 episodes × approximately 15 minutes.

## Season Question
Can two ordinary people become the parents this extraordinary child needs before Hell comes to take him back?

## Season Emotional Arc
Denial → adaptation → attachment → exposure → choice.

## Classical Structure

### Act I: The Family Forms (Episodes 1–3)
The parents receive the child, discover that normal explanations are failing and begin adapting to impossible parenting problems.

### Act II-A: The World Pushes Back (Episodes 4–5)
The baby’s powers affect school, neighbours and the wider community. The family’s secrecy and confidence are tested.

### Midpoint
The parents learn that the baby is not merely demonic. He is the son of Satan, and Hell knows where he is.

### Act II-B: The Family Fractures (Episodes 6–8)
External supernatural pressure and internal exhaustion create conflict between Mum and Dad. Satan begins communicating directly with the child.

### Act III: Hell Comes Home (Episodes 9–10)
The truth can no longer be contained. The family must choose whether Baby Demon is a threat to surrender or their son to protect.

## Episode Progression

### Episode 1 — Special Delivery
An exhausted couple receive the wrong baby after an administrative mix-up that may not be administrative at all. They accept increasingly disturbing signs as ordinary newborn behaviour until the baby’s first cry knocks out power across the neighbourhood.

**Parenting truth:** Nobody feels ready.
**Character turn:** They choose to keep him overnight.
**Hook:** Something beneath the house answers the baby’s cry.

### Episode 2 — Sleep Through the Night
The parents attempt every sleep-training method while the baby’s nightmares manifest physically in the house.

**Parenting truth:** Sleep deprivation breaks everyone.
**Character turn:** Dad stops treating the baby as a temporary problem.
**Hook:** The baby sleeps peacefully only when held over a glowing crack in the floor.

### Episode 3 — Teething
The baby’s first teeth arrive as fangs, household objects vanish and the family dog begins obeying him.

**Parenting truth:** Every milestone brings a new crisis.
**Character turn:** Mum rejects outside judgement and trusts her own instincts.
**Hook:** A symbol appears on the baby’s gum.

### Episode 4 — Playgroup
At a local baby group, an innocent nursery rhyme becomes an accidental summoning ritual.

**Parenting truth:** Parents compare themselves constantly.
**Character turn:** The couple become a functioning crisis team.
**Hook:** One summoned creature recognises the baby and kneels.

### Episode 5 — First Word
Everyone competes to make the baby say Mum or Dad. His actual first word is an ancient command that opens a portal.

**Parenting truth:** Parents attach enormous meaning to tiny moments.
**Character turn:** The parents learn who the child truly is.
**Midpoint revelation:** He is Satan’s son.

### Episode 6 — The Babysitter
Desperate for one normal night, the parents hire a fearless babysitter. The baby behaves perfectly until they leave.

**Parenting truth:** Parents need lives beyond parenting, then feel guilty for taking them.
**Character turn:** Mum and Dad admit how frightened and exhausted they are.
**Hook:** The babysitter returns speaking in an ancient voice.

### Episode 7 — Daddy Issues
Satan begins appearing through screens, mirrors and baby monitors, offering the child power and criticising the human father.

**Parenting truth:** Raising a child means confronting inherited family damage.
**Character turn:** Dad openly claims the child as his son.
**Hook:** Baby Demon reaches toward Satan’s image.

### Episode 8 — The Birthday Party
The parents stage a perfectly normal first birthday while Hell sends increasingly elaborate gifts.

**Parenting truth:** Parents perform happiness while barely holding things together.
**Character turn:** The family’s secret is nearly exposed.
**Hook:** The final gift is an invitation with a date.

### Episode 9 — The Child from Hell
The neighbourhood finally sees what Baby Demon can do after an emotional meltdown tears open the sky.

**Parenting truth:** Every parent fears being judged by their child’s worst moment.
**Character turn:** Mum and Dad fracture over whether keeping him is safe.
**Hook:** Satan arrives.

### Episode 10 — Family Day
Satan offers the parents safety, wealth and restored normality in exchange for his son. Baby Demon must choose between inherited destiny and the people who raised him.

**Parenting truth:** Love is choosing the child, not the fantasy of the child.
**Climax:** The parents stand against Hell together.
**Resolution:** Baby Demon chooses them.
**Season-two hook:** His choice awakens something older than Satan.

## Season Quality Test
- Each episode begins with a recognisable parenting challenge.
- Supernatural escalation grows from that challenge.
- The family relationship changes in every episode.
- Mythology advances without overwhelming comedy.
- Episode 10 resolves the emotional question while opening a larger world.
