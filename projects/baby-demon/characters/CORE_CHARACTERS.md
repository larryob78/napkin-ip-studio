# BABY DEMON — Core Character Bible

## Creative Rule
Every character must be funny because of who they are, not because the plot forces them to behave strangely. The supernatural chaos only works when the family relationships feel emotionally true.

---

## Baby Demon

### Role
The impossible child at the centre of the series.

### External Want
Love, food, attention, play and immediate satisfaction, like any baby.

### Internal Need
To feel that he belongs to this family, even when his nature makes him dangerous.

### Core Contradiction
He looks like the most terrifying creature ever born, but emotionally he is an affectionate, needy, curious baby.

### Comic Engine
Every normal developmental milestone becomes a supernatural catastrophe.

### Flaw
He has no control over his emotions or powers.

### Gift
He loves with absolute loyalty.

### Season Arc
He begins as a mysterious foundling whose powers appear accidental. Across the season, he starts making choices, protecting his parents and revealing that something in Hell expects him to return.

### Signature Behaviour
He purrs when happy, growls when tired and accidentally bends reality when frustrated.

### Visual Direction
Photoreal demonic infant. Small horns, ember-like eyes, tiny claws, imperfect baby teeth becoming fangs, subtle smoke and heat distortion when emotional. He must remain adorable in movement even when frightening in a still image.

---

## Mum

### Role
The emotional centre and primary believer in the family.

### External Want
To prove she can be a good mother and hold the family together.

### Internal Need
To accept that good parenting is not control. It is adaptation, honesty and love.

### Core Contradiction
She notices every parenting detail but refuses to accept the obvious supernatural explanation.

### Comic Engine
She interprets demonic behaviour through parenting books, online advice and maternal instinct.

### Flaw
She believes every problem can be solved if she tries harder.

### Strength
Fearless commitment to her child.

### Season Arc
She moves from anxious perfectionism to confident, unconventional motherhood. By the finale, she deliberately chooses Baby Demon over safety, reputation and social approval.

### Voice
Warm, quick, practical and increasingly exhausted.

---

## Dad

### Role
The pragmatic parent who uses denial as a survival mechanism.

### External Want
A normal family life with manageable problems.

### Internal Need
To stop retreating into jokes and take emotional responsibility.

### Core Contradiction
He is terrified of the baby but also completely besotted with him.

### Comic Engine
He explains supernatural events using absurdly ordinary logic, household fixes and improvised DIY.

### Flaw
Avoidance.

### Strength
He remains calm during chaos and becomes unexpectedly brave when his family is threatened.

### Season Arc
He begins as the parent most likely to suggest returning the baby. He ends the season openly claiming him as his son in front of Satan.

### Voice
Dry, understated and deadpan.

---

## Satan

### Role
The absent biological father and looming season antagonist.

### External Want
His son returned to Hell to fulfil a destiny.

### Internal Need
To understand why the child prefers an ordinary human family.

### Core Contradiction
He commands Hell but has no idea how to relate to his own baby.

### Comic Engine
Cosmic evil collides with divorced-parent energy, bruised ego and terrible co-parenting instincts.

### Flaw
Narcissism.

### Strength
He can be magnificent, persuasive and genuinely protective in ways that create moral ambiguity.

### Season Arc
He begins as a distant myth, becomes an intrusive presence and finally arrives in person. His failure to win the child becomes the emotional defeat of the season.

### Visual Direction
Elegant, cinematic and terrifying rather than cartoonishly evil. He should feel ancient, charismatic and slightly wounded.

---

## Core Family Dynamic
Mum wants to understand the baby. Dad wants to survive the baby. Baby wants both parents. Satan wants ownership. The series works when every supernatural conflict is also a family conflict.

---

## Character Quality Gate
Each episode must:
- Reveal a new side of at least one family member.
- Force a character to make a choice.
- Change a relationship, even slightly.
- Generate comedy from personality.
- Preserve Baby Demon as both frightening and lovable.
