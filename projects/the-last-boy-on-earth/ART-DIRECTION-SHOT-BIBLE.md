# THE LAST BOY ON EARTH — Art Direction Shot Bible
## Tencent Development Companion v0.1

**Status:** Confidential Development Material  
**Purpose:** Convert the approved visual direction into a coherent, repeatable image suite for the pitch deck and two-minute proof of concept.

## Visual thesis
A lonely child moves through the emotional archaeology of humanity with an alien expedition that slowly becomes his family. The imagery must be monumental enough for premium science fiction and intimate enough that every discovery changes a relationship.

**Balance:** 70% intimate archaeological mystery, 30% epic scale.

## Non-negotiable image standard
- Photographic credibility before spectacle.
- Hyper-real materials with age, moisture, weight, erosion and optical imperfection.
- Nature must have structurally transformed human architecture, not merely decorated it.
- The boy must remain emotionally readable at every scale.
- Alien design must feel biologically and culturally specific, never like a familiar grey alien.
- The Voyager Golden Record is the visual and emotional anchor.
- No frame should advertise the use of AI.

## Hero image suite
### 1. The arrival
**Frame:** Alien craft approaching a living, cloud-covered Earth from orbit.  
**Purpose:** Establish scale, awe and the promise of discovery.  
**Rule:** Earth must look abundant and alive, not post-apocalyptic.

### 2. The last boy
**Frame:** The boy held small in the foreground, facing a reclaimed city whose towers have become cliffs and ecosystems.  
**Purpose:** Communicate loneliness and visual ambition in one image.  
**Lens language:** Large-format wide composition with a physically believable foreground.

### 3. First contact
**Frame:** The boy and young alien scientist meeting at ground level, cautious hands and eye-lines carrying the emotion.  
**Purpose:** Sell the central friendship rather than alien spectacle.  
**Rule:** No weapons, heroic posing or sentimental glow.

### 4. The Golden Record
**Frame:** Macro close-up of the weathered Golden Record in soil, water or moss, followed by the boy holding it with reverence.  
**Purpose:** Create the recurring campaign symbol.  
**Palette:** Warm metallic gold against mineral grey, wet green and living earth.

### 5. Human archaeology
**Frame:** The crew inside an overgrown domestic or civic interior, examining one ordinary human object while the boy watches their misunderstanding.  
**Purpose:** Capture the episodic engine and gentle humour.  
**Rule:** The object must have emotional consequence, not function as decorative lore.

### 6. New family
**Frame:** The boy and expedition crew sharing a quiet meal, shelter or lakeside pause.  
**Purpose:** Prove the series can hold intimacy after scale.  
**Lens language:** 35mm–50mm observational framing, natural silence and restrained performance.

### 7. The hidden truth
**Frame:** The boy alone in a dark broadcast or archive space, illuminated by a human-language signal.  
**Purpose:** Introduce the season mystery without revealing its answer.  
**Rule:** Technology is tactile and functional, not generic holographic spectacle.

### 8. A new record
**Frame:** The boy and crew transmitting a new message from Earth as dawn crosses the reclaimed landscape.  
**Purpose:** Express hope earned through collective action.  
**Rule:** Emotional climax first, cosmic scale second.

## Character portrait suite
### The Boy
- Front portrait with direct, guarded eye contact
- Profile listening to recorded human voices
- Full-body silhouette showing repaired clothing and collected objects
- Vulnerable close-up after choosing trust

### Young Alien Scientist
- Humane eyes and original facial structure
- Curious posture without childlike caricature
- Tactile scientific tools rooted in alien culture
- Two-shot with the boy showing evolving equality

### Expedition Leader
- Composed authority, physical stillness and moral weight
- Portrait divided between mission light and Earth light

### Security Officer
- Intimidating silhouette contrasted with gentle physical care
- Protective action built from believable body weight

### Ship Intelligence
- Presence expressed through architecture, sound and controlled light rather than a generic floating face

## Environment families
1. Reclaimed city and flooded transport routes
2. Museum or archive containing emotionally charged artefacts
3. The boy's hidden home and survival systems
4. Broadcast facility and underground signal infrastructure
5. Alien craft interiors with culturally specific materials
6. Wild Earth landscapes where human geometry has nearly disappeared

Each environment family must support wide scale, medium character action and intimate detail shots.

## Cinematography rules
- Large-format wides for geography and awe
- 35mm–50mm for relationships and discovery
- 85mm for emotional portraits and material detail
- Slow reveals before action
- Motivated natural light, weather and atmospheric depth
- Camera height should respect the boy's point of view
- Figures may appear small in landscapes, but every wide must lead to an emotional close consequence

## Continuity and quality gates
- Same boy, clothing repairs, hair length and carried objects across every image
- Stable alien anatomy, eye shape, skin structure and movement logic
- Golden Record scale, markings, damage and reflectivity remain consistent
- Foliage follows ecological logic across matching locations
- Water, cloth, hands, contact and eye-lines receive manual review
- Avoid repeated background forms, synthetic texture and impossible architecture

## Two-minute proof image ladder
1. Orbital Earth
2. Reclaimed city reveal
3. Boy in concealment
4. First-contact close-up
5. Misunderstood human object
6. Golden Record music bridge
7. Crew and boy in shared frame
8. Final signal in darkness

## Canon boundaries
The following remain intentionally unresolved and must not be visually overcommitted before approval:
- Boy's final name and exact age
- Exact mechanism of his survival
- Final alien culture and authority system
- Cause of humanity's disappearance
- Identity and design of the incoming opposing force

## Change record
**v0.1:** Added a production-ready hero-image suite, portrait plan, environment families, cinematography rules, continuity gates and proof-of-concept image ladder derived from the locked pitch canon.

## Weakest remaining art-direction area
The alien expedition still needs one locked design grammar covering anatomy, clothing, tools, ship materials and cultural motifs. Until that system is approved, individual alien images risk looking impressive but unrelated.
