# THE LAST BOY ON EARTH
## Canon v0.1

**Format:** Premium episodic animated science-fiction adventure  
**Season:** 10 episodes × approximately 15 minutes  
**Status:** Confidential Development Material

## Locked premise

Centuries after humanity disappears, an alien expedition reaches Earth by following the legacy of the Voyager Golden Record. Expecting to discover a civilisation, they find a single surviving boy. Together, they explore an Earth reclaimed by nature and uncover what humanity was, what happened to it and what may still be worth saving.

## Locked genre and tone

- Science-fiction adventure
- Emotional, hopeful and visually cinematic
- Wonder before action
- Mystery unfolds through discovery
- Loss is present, but the series is not nihilistic
- Friendship is the emotional engine

## Human truth

Every child wonders whether they are alone.

Every generation wonders what kind of world it will leave behind.

The series asks what is truly worth preserving about humanity: not simply technology or monuments, but stories, kindness, curiosity, music, courage and love.

## Core episodic engine

1. The boy and aliens enter a new human place or follow a clue.
2. The aliens misunderstand an aspect of humanity.
3. The boy's memory or belief is challenged.
4. A discovery advances the mystery of humanity's disappearance.
5. A relationship changes.
6. The episode ends with renewed hope, danger or a larger question.

## Visual canon

- Earth is breathtaking, overgrown and reclaimed by nature
- Familiar human objects become archaeological mysteries
- The alien design must feel original, humane and non-clichéd
- The Voyager Golden Record is a major emotional and visual symbol
- Cinematic realism, scale and tactile detail
- Avoid generic flying saucers, sterile sci-fi corridors and familiar grey-alien language

## Story guardrails

- The boy cannot remain passive or merely serve as a guide.
- The aliens need competing personalities, goals and flaws.
- Every discovery must affect character, not just provide lore.
- Humanity must be presented honestly, including beauty and failure.
- Mystery reveals must be earned and causally connected.
- Hope should emerge from choices, not sentimental speeches.

## Open development questions

- Boy's name, age, history and survival mechanism
- Whether he truly is the last human
- Alien expedition roster and internal conflict
- Exact timeline after humanity's disappearance
- Nature of the extinction or departure mystery
- Why the Golden Record signal became discoverable
- Season antagonist or opposing force
- Final revelation and Season Two launch point
