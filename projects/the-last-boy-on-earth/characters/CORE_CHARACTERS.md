# THE LAST BOY ON EARTH — Core Character Bible

## Creative Rule
The series is not about solving the extinction of humanity as quickly as possible. It is about a lonely child and a group of outsiders learning what family, memory and humanity mean.

---

## The Boy

### Working Name
To be locked after cultural and market review.

### Age Range
Approximately 11–13.

### Role
The last known human child and emotional centre of the series.

### External Want
To discover whether anyone else survived.

### Internal Need
To stop defining himself only through loss and allow himself to belong to a new family.

### Core Contradiction
He knows more about surviving Earth than anyone alive, but very little about living with other people.

### Flaw
He hides fear behind independence and distrust.

### Gift
Curiosity, empathy and an instinctive ability to see value in broken things.

### Emotional Wound
He believes everyone he loves eventually leaves.

### Season Arc
He begins convinced that survival means depending on nobody. Through the alien crew, he gradually risks trust. In the finale, he chooses connection over isolation even when doing so may cost him the only home he remembers.

### Signature Behaviour
He records messages for people who may never hear them and collects small human objects whose purpose the aliens misunderstand.

### Visual Direction
Real, weathered and resilient rather than post-apocalyptic costume cliché. Clothes repaired many times. Personal objects carry emotional history.

---

## Expedition Leader

### Role
Commander of the alien mission and reluctant guardian.

### External Want
Complete the mission, understand humanity and return the crew safely.

### Internal Need
To accept that discovery creates responsibility.

### Core Contradiction
Highly rational but increasingly guided by affection for the boy.

### Flaw
Emotional restraint and excessive loyalty to protocol.

### Gift
Calm authority, moral courage and the capacity to change.

### Season Arc
Begins treating the boy as a historic discovery. Ends treating him as family and defying mission orders to protect him.

---

## Young Alien Scientist

### Role
The boy’s first true friend.

### External Want
Understand human culture and prove their scientific ability.

### Internal Need
Learn that people cannot be reduced to evidence and data.

### Core Contradiction
Knows thousands of facts about humanity but misunderstands basic emotions.

### Flaw
Intrusive curiosity.

### Gift
Wonder, optimism and intellectual bravery.

### Season Arc
Moves from studying the boy to standing beside him. Their friendship becomes the heart of the series.

### Comic and Emotional Engine
Misreads ordinary human objects, customs, humour and music, while occasionally understanding the boy better than the adults do.

---

## Security Officer

### Role
Protector, sceptic and physical counterweight to the group.

### External Want
Keep the crew safe from an unknown planet and unknown human.

### Internal Need
Recognise that vulnerability is not the same as danger.

### Core Contradiction
Looks intimidating but becomes the most gentle member of the boy’s new family.

### Flaw
Assumes the worst to prevent catastrophe.

### Gift
Absolute loyalty once trust is earned.

### Season Arc
Begins seeing the boy as a biological and strategic risk. Ends risking their life to save him.

---

## Ship Intelligence

### Role
The expedition’s artificial intelligence, navigator and keeper of mission records.

### External Want
Complete the original mission parameters.

### Internal Need
Develop a definition of life and value that extends beyond instructions.

### Core Contradiction
Has access to humanity’s recorded history but has never experienced a human relationship.

### Flaw
Literal obedience.

### Gift
Perfect memory and an emerging capacity for compassion.

### Season Arc
Its interpretation of the Voyager Golden Record evolves from data archive to emotional testimony. It may ultimately conceal information to protect the boy.

---

## Central Relationship Map
- The Boy + Young Scientist: friendship and mutual discovery.
- The Boy + Expedition Leader: child and reluctant guardian.
- The Boy + Security Officer: mistrust becoming protection.
- The Boy + Ship Intelligence: memory, truth and the question of what counts as human.
- The Crew + Earth: archaeology slowly becoming belonging.

---

## Character Quality Gate
Each episode must:
- Advance at least one relationship.
- Reveal humanity through a specific object, place, memory or choice.
- Force the boy to choose between isolation and connection.
- Give an alien character a changed understanding of humanity.
- Balance loss with wonder and hope.
