# THE LAST BOY ON EARTH
## Canon and Continuity Review v0.1

**Status:** active editorial control document  
**Primary manuscript:** `GREENLIGHT_BOOK.md`  
**Locked canon authority:** `CANON.md`

## Purpose

This review records conflicts, resolves what can be resolved without founder approval, and prevents provisional development ideas from silently becoming canon during layout, image generation or motion testing.

## Resolution hierarchy

1. `CANON.md` wins where it is explicit.
2. `GREENLIGHT_BOOK.md` is the canonical editorial manuscript for external outputs.
3. Dedicated treatment, character, world and visual files provide deeper source detail.
4. Where source files disagree on an unlocked choice, the choice remains provisional until deliberately approved.

---

# 1. FORMAT / VISUAL LANGUAGE

## Conflict found

`CANON.md` explicitly defines the project as a **premium episodic animated science-fiction adventure**. Several visual-development files use phrases such as “premium live-action credibility” or “live-action credibility benchmark.”

## Resolution

**Locked format remains animated.**

“Live-action” language is not a format change. It is reinterpreted only as a craft benchmark for photographic discipline: believable lenses, natural light, tactile materials, physical scale, restrained camera behaviour and credible environmental interaction.

Visual-production files should therefore use language such as:

> Premium animated feature-film credibility, using live-action photographic discipline as a cinematography and rendering benchmark.

This preserves the locked animated format while retaining the intended high-end cinematic realism.

**Status:** resolved without changing locked canon.

---

# 2. BOY: LAST HUMAN VS LAST KNOWN HUMAN

## Potential ambiguity

The locked premise says the expedition finds a single surviving boy, while the season architecture later reveals off-world human communities. `CANON.md` also explicitly leaves open whether he truly is the last human.

## Resolution

External editorial copy should use **“last known human child on Earth”** or equivalent language when precision is needed. “The Last Boy on Earth” remains the title and emotional framing, not proof that no humans survive elsewhere.

Do not state that he is the final living human in the universe.

**Status:** resolved as wording discipline; underlying mystery remains open.

# 3. BOY AGE / IDENTITY

## Source state

`CANON.md` leaves age and history open. Character development recommends an age range of approximately 11–13, with twelve as the strongest playing-age recommendation.

## Resolution

- final name: open
- exact age: open
- proposal-safe range: approximately 11–13
- recommended playing age: 12, explicitly provisional
- carers, family structure, ethnicity, accent, birthplace and exact isolation duration: open

No image prompt should introduce an exact canonical age or biography unless approved.

**Status:** controlled, not locked.

---

# 4. MEMORY ARK

## Source state

The Memory Ark is the strongest current causal mythology framework, but it is not locked in `CANON.md`.

## Resolution

Every external occurrence must remain labelled **recommended**, **provisional**, **current framework**, or equivalent until founder approval.

It may be used to test episode causality, visual design and the two-minute proof only where the proof does not require the audience to accept it as final canon.

The boy must never become a biological key, chosen one, unique genetic credential or magical activation device.

**Status:** provisional by design.

# 6. TIME SCALE / ECOLOGICAL RETURN

## Locked fact

`CANON.md` says the story takes place **centuries after humanity disappears**.

## Editorial rule

Avoid casual “decades later” phrasing in final external copy or visual prompts unless it describes a local process rather than the total elapsed time. Preferred language: **centuries of ecological return**, **long ecological succession**, or simply **reclaimed Earth**.

The exact year and precise number of centuries remain open.

**Status:** locked broad scale, exact date open.

---

# 7. HUMANITY'S DISAPPEARANCE

## Locked / open boundary

Humanity is absent from organised civilisation on Earth. The exact cause, timing, mechanism and whether departure and catastrophe overlap remain open.

## Resolution

World design may show uneven abandonment, long-term ecological succession, maintained systems and deliberate concealment. It must not introduce one universal burn layer, mass-grave logic, invasion insignia or other image that silently fixes the cause.

**Status:** open.

---

# 8. OFF-WORLD HUMANS

## Current manuscript state

Season One reveals separated off-world human communities as part of the current final-act architecture.

## Canon caution

`CANON.md` leaves the final revelation and whether the boy is truly the last human open. Therefore the existence, number, culture and politics of off-world communities must be treated as **current season architecture pending final founder lock**, not retroactively rewritten into base canon.

For pitch purposes, this reveal may remain in the Greenlight Book as the current recommended Season One payoff, provided Page 38 and the open-decisions register continue to mark the larger details as unresolved.

**Status:** current recommended season architecture, not base-canon lock.

---

# 9. ALIEN CULTURE / SPECIES

## Current state

The shared-memory culture, provisional anatomy, gesture grammar, clothing logic, tools and listening-ship design create a coherent development system.

## Resolution

The **cultural principle** may guide visual tests, but names, exact anatomy, public institution, authority structure, rank markers and final ship materials remain provisional.

No generated portrait becomes canon simply because it is visually successful. Only assets explicitly marked `APPROVED` may become continuity reference.

**Status:** development-controlled.

---

# 10. TWO-MINUTE PROOF BOUNDARY

The proof should demonstrate:

- Golden Record arrival premise
- reclaimed Dublin
- boy and scientist relationship
- alien originality
- humour through cultural misunderstanding
- one earned act of trust
- a final signal / mystery hook

It should **not** require final answers about:

- disappearance cause
- Memory Ark architecture
- off-world settlement politics
- approaching-force identity
- boy's exact biography

This keeps the proof useful even if mythology changes later.

**Status:** locked production guardrail for current development.

---

# ACTIONS FROM THIS REVIEW

1. Keep `CANON.md` unchanged.
2. Remove format ambiguity from visual-production wording by preserving animation and treating photographic realism as a craft benchmark.
3. Keep Memory Ark labelling provisional in all external outputs.
4. Keep episode titles explicitly working until founder lock.
5. Use “last known human child” where literal survival claims matter.
6. Avoid “decades after humanity” wording; broad canon is centuries.
7. Build the two-minute proof around relationship and premise, not unresolved mythology.

---

# REVIEW RESULT

No locked canon needs to be overwritten.

The manuscript is structurally coherent enough to continue toward layout and proof development. The largest remaining founder decisions are the final status of the Memory Ark framework, the boy's identity details, and whether the current off-world-human reveal is formally promoted into locked Season One canon.
