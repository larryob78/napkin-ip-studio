## Series synopsis

Centuries after humanity vanishes from Earth, a small Vael expedition follows a hidden pattern encoded in the mathematical and musical language of the Voyager Golden Record. They expect to find the civilisation that once introduced itself to the universe through voices, music and images. Instead, they find a breathtaking planet reclaimed by nature and Eli Byrne, a wary twelve-year-old who has survived alone beneath the ruins of Dublin for eighteen months. Eli knows the safe paths through the flooded streets and living buildings, but he refuses to follow the repeating signal that has haunted him since his refuge failed. He believes it can only confirm the truth he fears most: humanity escaped and deliberately left him behind.

As Eli and the expedition search Earth’s ruins, each discovery changes their understanding of humanity and of one another. Tavi, the young scientist who recognised the signal, becomes Eli’s first true friend; Commander Sela begins to value responsibility over protocol; Orro’s suspicion turns into fierce protection; and ARO, the ship intelligence, learns that preserving memory means little without preserving people. The signal eventually reveals the Chorus, a dormant human network that can lead Eli to Haven, a concealed settlement of survivors beyond Earth. But reopening it may also summon the Quiet, an ancient containment system that once forced humanity to flee. The season becomes a choice between remaining safely alone and risking everything to be found, as Eli and his new family decide what honest record of humanity deserves to travel forward.
