# THE LAST BOY ON EARTH — Season One Map

## Format
10 episodes × approximately 15 minutes.

## Season Question
Can the last human child trust a group of aliens enough to stop merely surviving and begin living again?

## Season Emotional Arc
Isolation → curiosity → friendship → distrust → sacrifice → belonging.

## Classical Structure

### Act I: The Discovery (Episodes 1–3)
The alien expedition arrives following the Voyager Golden Record, discovers the boy and begins exploring a reclaimed Earth with him.

### Act II-A: What Humanity Left Behind (Episodes 4–5)
Each discovery complicates the crew’s idealised view of humanity. The boy begins to trust them while withholding the truth about his past.

### Midpoint
Evidence suggests the boy may not be the last human after all.

### Act II-B: Hope Becomes Dangerous (Episodes 6–8)
The search for survivors divides the crew, exposes hidden mission orders and forces the boy to confront the possibility that his loneliness was not accidental.

### Act III: The Choice (Episodes 9–10)
The crew uncovers the truth behind humanity’s disappearance and must choose between completing their mission and protecting the boy’s future.

## Episode Progression

### Episode 1 — The Signal
An alien expedition reaches Earth expecting the civilisation that sent the Golden Record. Instead, in the ruins beneath a vast abandoned city, they find one living boy.

**Human truth:** Being found can be as frightening as being lost.
**Character turn:** The boy chooses not to run.
**Hook:** He recognises the music playing from the Golden Record.

### Episode 2 — The Visitors
The crew tries to communicate while the boy tests whether they can be trusted. A simple meal becomes the first fragile bridge between them.

**Human truth:** Trust begins through small acts.
**Character turn:** The young scientist stops treating the boy as a specimen.
**Hook:** The boy reveals a map covered in unexplained signals.

### Episode 3 — Museum of Everything
The boy leads the crew through a ruined museum where the aliens misunderstand human artefacts and the boy confronts memories he has avoided.

**Human truth:** Objects matter because of the people attached to them.
**Character turn:** The security officer protects something emotionally valuable rather than strategically useful.
**Hook:** A recently moved object proves someone else may have been there.

### Episode 4 — The City That Grew Back
The group crosses an overgrown city where nature and human infrastructure have fused into a dangerous new ecosystem.

**Human truth:** The world continues after us.
**Character turn:** The expedition leader breaks protocol to save the boy.
**Hook:** They receive a repeating transmission in a human language.

### Episode 5 — A Voice in the Dark
The crew follows the transmission to an underground archive. The message appears to be recent and addressed to the boy by name.

**Human truth:** Hope can reopen old wounds.
**Character turn:** The boy admits he has heard the signal before.
**Midpoint revelation:** He may not be the last human.

### Episode 6 — Home
The signal leads to the place where the boy lived with his family. The crew discovers fragments of his past and evidence that he was deliberately hidden.

**Human truth:** Home is memory as much as place.
**Character turn:** The boy lets the young scientist see his grief.
**Hook:** Mission records reveal the expedition knew more about Earth than it admitted.

### Episode 7 — The Golden Lie
The ship intelligence uncovers sealed orders: the expedition was sent not only to study humanity, but to retrieve something hidden on Earth.

**Human truth:** Trust collapses when truth is withheld.
**Character turn:** The boy leaves the crew.
**Hook:** The hidden object may be connected to humanity’s disappearance.

### Episode 8 — The Last Broadcast
Alone, the boy follows the signal into a dangerous broadcast facility while the divided crew decides whether to obey orders or rescue him.

**Human truth:** Independence can become another form of fear.
**Character turn:** The security officer openly defies the mission to protect him.
**Hook:** The broadcast reveals a human settlement beyond Earth.

### Episode 9 — Departure
The crew learns that activating the hidden technology could reveal the boy’s location to forces that destroyed or displaced humanity.

**Human truth:** Hope always carries risk.
**Character turn:** The boy chooses to trust the crew with the final decision.
**Hook:** Their pursuers enter the solar system.

### Episode 10 — A New Record
The crew prepares to flee, but the boy refuses to let humanity’s story end as a warning or a ruin. Together they create and transmit a new record of Earth.

**Human truth:** Legacy is what we choose to send forward.
**Climax:** The expedition sacrifices its original mission to protect the boy.
**Resolution:** He leaves Earth with a new family, carrying human stories with him.
**Season-two hook:** A human voice answers the new transmission.

---

## Season Quality Test
- Each episode contains a physical discovery and an emotional discovery.
- The mystery advances through character choices, not convenient exposition.
- Earth remains beautiful, dangerous and specific.
- The aliens develop distinct views of humanity.
- The finale resolves the boy’s isolation while expanding the universe.
