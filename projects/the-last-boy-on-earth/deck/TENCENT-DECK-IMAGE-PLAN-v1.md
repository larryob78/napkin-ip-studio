# THE LAST BOY ON EARTH
## Tencent Deck Image Plan v1

**Purpose:** Create a consistent full-bleed 16:9 image system for the ten-page Series Development Proposal.

## Global Visual Rules

- 16:9 landscape, composed for full-bleed PowerPoint use.
- Minimum working resolution: 3840 × 2160.
- Premium photoreal cinematic science fiction.
- Reclaimed future Dublin, not generic post-apocalypse.
- Natural Irish weather, volumetric atmosphere, practical-feeling light.
- Palette: wet green, mineral grey, deep blue, living earth, warm Golden Record accents.
- Camera language: large-format landscapes; 35–50mm relationship scenes; 85mm portraits.
- Characters must remain recognisable across all images.
- Keep all critical faces and story information inside the central safe area.
- No embedded text, logos, watermarks, neon cyberpunk, sterile chrome, generic saucers or plastic skin.

# PAGE 1 — COVER

## Hero Image
Eli and Tavi stand small in the foreground on an overgrown Dublin height, looking across a vast reclaimed city as the Vael craft descends through low cloud. A subtle warm-gold circular echo of the Golden Record appears through reflected light, not as a magical symbol.

**Composition:** clean negative space upper-left for title. 24–35mm lens. Epic scale, intimate body language.

**Proof:** the series hook in one frame—last boy, alien arrival, reclaimed Earth, wonder rather than invasion.

# Character Continuity Requirements

## Eli Byrne
- Age 12.
- Real, weathered, resilient; no action-hero styling.
- Repaired layered clothing, practical backpack, one identifiable personal object.
- Same hair, face proportions, clothing family and age across all pages.

## Tavi Renn
- Humane but unmistakably non-human.
- Vael design grammar: low centre of gravity, two primary arms plus smaller fine-motor limbs, subtle mineral translucency, layered woven membrane clothing.
- Readable curiosity without humanising the anatomy into costume makeup.

## Sela Venn
- Calm authority, economical movement, visibly maintained service garments.

## Orro Kest
- Strongest physical silhouette; intimidating at first glance, gentle in precise gestures.

## ARO
- Integrated into material, sound, texture and ship architecture. Never a floating blue person.

---

# Approval Gates

An image is rejected if:
- Eli’s age or face drifts.
- Vael anatomy changes between frames.
- Dublin could be mistaken for any generic city.
- Nature appears as decorative vines rather than structural transformation.
- The image looks glossy, game-like, cartoonish or generically AI-generated.
- Eye-lines, hands, scale, cloth, water or foliage fail under presentation viewing.
- It reveals provisional mythology as settled fact.
- It lacks a clear story purpose for its assigned page.
