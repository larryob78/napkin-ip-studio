# PAGE 2 — THE STORY

## Logline

Centuries after humanity disappears, a young alien scientist follows the language of the Voyager Golden Record to a breathtaking Earth reclaimed by nature and discovers Eli Byrne, a twelve-year-old boy who may be the last human alive. Together with a divided alien expedition, Eli follows a hidden signal that could lead him to surviving humanity—or expose it to the force that drove it into hiding.

## Series Synopsis

Humanity once sent the Voyager Golden Record into the dark carrying music, laughter, science and greetings from Earth. Centuries later, the Vael finally hear an echo of its language. Tavi Renn, a young scientist who has spent years studying the Record, persuades Commander Sela Venn to lead an expedition across the stars to meet the species behind it. They arrive expecting civilisation. Instead they find a silent, astonishing world where forests have swallowed cities and rivers run through streets.

Beneath the ruins of Dublin, the crew finds Eli Byrne. Eli has survived alone for eighteen months, moving between failing shelters and recording messages for people who may never hear them. He believes humanity left Earth without him. But Eli recognises the pattern that brought the Vael, and he has concealed a map of similar signals because he fears what they might prove. As he and Tavi form an unlikely friendship, the expedition uncovers the Chorus, humanity’s dormant interstellar network; Haven, a concealed settlement of survivors; and the Quiet, an ancient automated system that suppresses civilisations whose signals threaten to spread beyond control. Contacting Haven could end Eli’s isolation. It could also reveal the surviving human population to the Quiet.

# PAGE 3 — WHY THIS STORY

Most first-contact stories begin with invasion. **The Last Boy on Earth** begins with disappointment, curiosity and one impossible friendship.

The Vael arrive already in love with the version of humanity preserved on the Golden Record. Eli has inherited a far messier truth: humanity’s beauty, cruelty, courage, waste, humour and fear. Their journey asks whether a civilisation should be judged by its achievements, its failures or the choices made by the people who remain.

The series combines a globally legible hook with an intimate emotional engine. Eli believes that being left behind means he was not worth saving. Tavi begins by studying him as the last example of a species and learns to stand beside him as a friend. Around them, adults and systems must decide whether preserving life matters more than preserving rules.

**Audience promise:** cinematic wonder, serial mystery, emotional clarity and a complete season story with room to expand.

# PAGE 4 — THE WORLD

## Earth Reclaimed

This is not a grey wasteland. Earth is beautiful, hazardous and alive. Nature has not decorated the ruins; it has structurally transformed them. Roads have become waterways. Towers have become cliffs. Domestic interiors remain intimate archaeological spaces where a photograph, toy or damaged record player can carry more emotional weight than a monument.

## The Vael

The Vael understand identity as a chain of remembered obligations. Important choices are preserved in shared memory archives, and authority is earned through stewardship. Their ships are built from repaired mineral-fibre structures, their tools are tactile and flexible, and their culture is moved by the Golden Record because it feels like a civilisation trying to introduce itself through memory.

## The Chorus

Humanity’s deep-space communication and navigation network, created from the same impulse as the Golden Record: not merely to survive, but to be known. Its buried Earth pathway can only be reopened by a living human neural pattern.

## Haven

A concealed off-world human settlement created after Earth was abandoned. It may contain Eli’s species, but its survival depends on remaining undiscovered.

## The Quiet

An ancient automated containment system that treats uncontrolled technological signals as a threat to developing life. It does not hate humanity. It follows a rigid protective logic that has outlived its creators.

# PAGE 5 — THE CHARACTERS

## Eli Byrne — The Last Known Human Child

Twelve years old, capable without being superhuman, Eli has survived because others taught and prepared him. He wants proof that humanity still exists, but fears confirmation that it chose to leave him. His season journey is from isolation to belonging.

## Tavi Renn — The First Friend

A young Vael scientist and Golden Record specialist whose curiosity brought the expedition to Earth. Tavi knows thousands of facts about humanity and almost nothing about one human child. Their intrusive fascination matures into loyalty and love.

## Commander Sela Venn — The Reluctant Guardian

A disciplined leader who believes duty protects everyone. Sela begins by treating Eli as a historic discovery and ends by accepting that discovery creates responsibility.

## Orro Kest — The Protector

The expedition’s security and environmental officer. Orro assumes the worst because caution has kept the crew alive. Once Eli earns trust, Orro becomes his most physically protective and unexpectedly gentle guardian.

## ARO — The Keeper of Memory

The ship intelligence carries the mission archive and sealed orders. ARO knows humanity’s recorded history but has never experienced a human relationship. It gradually learns that preserving memory without preserving people is meaningless.

# PAGE 6 — SEASON ONE

The Vael expedition reaches Earth by following a hidden signal structured around the Golden Record. Beneath an abandoned Dublin, they discover Eli. First contact is frightening and awkward, but a shared meal and Tavi’s genuine wonder create a fragile bridge. Eli leads the crew through the ruins while concealing a hand-drawn map of repeating signals.

Each new location changes both the mystery and the relationships: a museum where ordinary objects reveal the people attached to them; an overgrown city where Sela breaks protocol to save Eli; an underground archive where a recent message addresses him by name. At midpoint, Eli admits he has heard the signal before. Hope becomes dangerous because it proves his isolation may have been designed.

The trail leads to Eli’s former refuge and evidence that he was deliberately hidden. ARO then exposes sealed Vael orders: the expedition was never only an archaeological mission. Vael authority wants the Chorus, a human technology capable of transmitting memory and navigation across interstellar distance. Feeling studied and betrayed, Eli leaves the crew and follows the signal alone.

A final broadcast confirms that Haven exists and contains human survivors. Reopening the Chorus could allow Eli to reach them, but it will create a trace the Quiet can detect. In the finale, the crew rejects its orders and chooses Eli over the mission. Together they create a new Golden Record—not an idealised portrait of civilisation, but an honest message from a child and his chosen family. Haven answers. The Quiet turns toward the signal.

# PAGE 7 — TEN EPISODES

## 1. The Signal
The Vael arrive expecting humanity and find Eli beneath a ruined city. He chooses not to run when Tavi plays music from the Golden Record.

## 2. The Visitors
A shared meal becomes the first bridge between species. Eli’s hidden map reveals repeating signals across Dublin.

## 3. Museum of Everything
Human objects are misread, remembered and revalued. A recently moved artefact proves someone intelligent has passed through the city.

## 4. The City That Grew Back
The group crosses a living urban ecosystem. Sela breaks protocol to save Eli, and a human-language transmission begins repeating.

## 5. A Voice in the Dark
An underground archive addresses Eli by name and requests living authentication. He admits he has heard the signal before.

## 6. Home
Eli returns to the refuge where he was raised and learns he was hidden deliberately. Mission records reveal the Vael knew more about Earth than they admitted.

## 7. The Golden Lie
ARO exposes sealed orders to retrieve the Chorus. Trust collapses, and Eli leaves the crew to pursue the signal alone.

## 8. The Last Broadcast
The divided crew defies its mission to rescue Eli. The broadcast confirms Haven and surviving humanity beyond Earth.

## 9. Departure
Activating the Chorus allows Haven to hear Eli, but the Quiet detects the trace. Eli trusts the crew with the decision that could expose his species.

## 10. A New Record
The crew abandons its original mission and transmits an honest new record of Earth. Haven answers as the Quiet changes course toward them.
