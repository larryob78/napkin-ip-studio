# 1. Gate objective

The project does not need five attractive concept images. It needs five **reusable identity authorities** that can survive portrait, full body, wide shot, rain, interaction, motion and emotional close-up without redesign.

The Identity Gate is passed only when the following references are approved as a coherent family:

1. **REF01 — Boy front portrait**
2. **REF02 — Boy full body + repaired wardrobe**
3. **REF03 — Young scientist front / three-quarter portrait**
4. **REF04 — Scientist full-body silhouette + field recorder**
5. **REF05 — Security officer silhouette + protective tool**

Until all five pass, do not generate final storyboard frames. Exploratory frames may be used only to expose design weaknesses.

---

# 2. Canon protection

Visual approval is not permission to silently lock unresolved story facts.

## Locked for production behaviour

- the boy is the last known human child on Earth
- he is recognisably a child, observant, capable and wary
- the young scientist is the boy's emotional co-lead and must be capable of subtle performance
- the security officer initially reads as formidable but must later support gentleness and protection
- alien technology is tactile, culturally coherent and non-generic
- wardrobe, tools and bodies must work in a premium episodic animated world with photographic credibility

## Still provisional

- boy's final name
- exact age
- ethnicity and family history
- exact duration alone
- final signature personal object
- alien personal names
- final species anatomy
- exact alien institutional structure
- final cultural symbolism not already approved in canon

**Production rule:** approve what the camera needs while leaving biography and mythology open.

# 3. Approval order

Use this order to minimise expensive rework.

## Gate A — faces

**A1. REF01 Boy portrait**  
Lock face, hair family, skin material, emotional range and baseline wardrobe collar/strap details.

**A2. REF03 Young scientist portrait**  
Lock the alien face only after testing performance, not novelty. The scientist must be unmistakably non-human but capable of warmth, embarrassment, concentration and concern.

Do not proceed if either face requires prompt reinvention to produce a new expression.

## Gate B — bodies and carried systems

**B1. REF02 Boy full body**  
Propagate the approved REF01 face. Lock proportions, silhouette, wardrobe construction, footwear and field bag.

**B2. REF04 Scientist full body + recorder**  
Propagate the approved REF03 face. Lock silhouette, anatomy-to-costume integration and recorder scale.

## Gate C — contrast character

**C1. REF05 Security officer**  
Design only after REF03/04 establish the civilisation's body, clothing, repair and tool grammar. The officer must feel from the same culture without reading like a bulked-up version of the scientist.

When REF01–05 pass, freeze them as the source set for REF06, REF13 and REF14.

# 4. Global generation rules

Every candidate must preserve the following.

## Camera and rendering

- premium episodic animation with photographic credibility
- real 35–85mm lens behaviour
- restrained depth of field
- physically plausible light falloff
- optical imperfection rather than synthetic hyper-sharpness
- tactile skin, cloth, mineral fibre, wet surfaces and repaired materials
- no fashion-editorial gloss
- no videogame character-select lighting

## Character direction

- natural asymmetry
- neutral, useful poses before heroic poses
- readable hands
- readable eye-lines
- emotional restraint
- no generic “AI beautiful” faces
- no exaggerated sadness, menace or cuteness

## Alien civilisation continuity

- layered repairable garments
- quiet mineral greys, oxidised greens, deep desaturated blues, bone-neutral layers
- warm amber only for memory/reply states
- no neon cyberpunk
- no floating blue holograms
- no military insignia shorthand
- tools reveal function through physical layers, orientation and contact

## Earth continuity where visible

- wet Atlantic atmosphere
- restrained reclaimed-Dublin cue
- nature has structural logic
- no generic apocalypse grime

---

# 5. Candidate production method

For each identity reference, generate a **controlled contact sheet**, not one-off hero images.

## Pass 1 — silhouette / face search

Generate 8 candidates from one brief with only small controlled variation.

Change only one or two dimensions at a time:

- facial geometry
- hair treatment
- eye shape
- body proportion
- garment panel arrangement
- tool silhouette

Do not change camera, world, costume category and mood simultaneously. That makes comparison meaningless.

## Pass 2 — convergence

Select the strongest 2 candidates and generate 4 controlled refinements of each.

The goal is not “more beautiful.” Test:

- expression range
- left/right three-quarter consistency
- hands
- profile
- neutral mouth
- eye-line accuracy
- material continuity

## Pass 3 — stress test

The winning candidate must survive at least four states before approval:

1. neutral reference state
2. emotional close-up
3. full-body or silhouette
4. interaction / movement state

If the face or body changes materially between these tests, return to REVIEW.

# 6. REF01 — BOY FRONT PORTRAIT

**Release priority:** first  
**Approval state at start:** WIP  
**Downstream:** REF02, REF06, REF13, REF14 and most proof shots.

## Production brief

Create the definitive visual identity of the last known human child on Earth. He is approximately early-adolescent in read but the image must not force an exact age. He has survived through routine, observation and maintained systems rather than action-hero violence. He is wary of people, not frightened of the physical world.

## Master prompt

**REF01 MASTER**  
Premium episodic animated science-fiction character reference with photographic credibility. Child-eye-level 85mm-equivalent portrait, head and upper torso of the last known human boy on a reclaimed future Earth. Observant before expressive, emotionally contained, capable and watchful, still unmistakably a child. Direct eye contact that feels cautious rather than defiant. Natural asymmetry, tactile skin and hair, weather exposure without brutality, repaired weatherproof outer layer over a softer older inner garment, one practical field-bag strap entering frame. Quiet wet Atlantic daylight, restrained reclaimed civic background falling softly out of focus, physically plausible lens behaviour, subtle optical imperfection, believable cloth weight and repairs, no heroic posing, no sentimentality, no dystopian fashion styling.

## Negative / rejection language

Reject if any of the following appears:

- adult facial proportions
- model-like grooming or fashion photography
- tactical armour
- fantasy hero styling
- heavy grime used as shorthand for hardship
- over-large emotional eyes
- obvious “chosen one” symbolism
- exaggerated scars
- exact biographical symbolism not in canon
- synthetic skin smoothing
- extreme cinematic colour grade

## Required contact-sheet outputs

- neutral front
- slight left three-quarter
- slight right three-quarter
- neutral closed mouth
- suppressed amusement
- alert recognition
- concern without fear

## Approval freeze

On approval record:

- face geometry
- eye shape/colour family
- brow shape
- hairline, length and texture
- nose/mouth geometry
- skin material family
- approved asymmetries
- exact outer-collar / inner-layer treatment visible in portrait

**File target:** `LBOE_PROOF_REF01_BOY-PORTRAIT_v##_APPROVED.png`

# 7. REF03 — YOUNG SCIENTIST PORTRAIT

**Release priority:** second  
**Approval state at start:** WIP  
**Downstream:** REF04, REF06, REF13, REF14 and the emotional centre of the proof.

## Production brief

The scientist must be instantly alien yet playable like a real screen character. Novel anatomy is useful only if it preserves subtle eye-lines, listening, embarrassment, humour and concern.

## Master prompt

**REF03 MASTER**  
Premium episodic animated science-fiction character reference with photographic credibility. Matched front and three-quarter 85mm-equivalent portraits of a young alien scientist, emotionally intelligent and intensely curious, unmistakably non-human but fully capable of subtle performance. Test a subtly widened upper skull, large expressive eyes that are not childlike, quiet mineral skin quality, narrow flexible sound-sensitive ear structures, fine secondary eye membrane visible only as a biological detail, attentive forward-balanced posture. Layered repairable field clothing in weathered mineral grey, oxidised green and deep desaturated blue, culturally specific seams and visible tension points, no armour. Soft physically motivated ship or Earth-side light, tactile skin and textile detail, natural asymmetry, intelligence before innocence, warmth without cuteness, no familiar grey-alien shorthand.

## Negative / rejection language

Reject:

- grey-alien archetype
- human face with cosmetic prosthetics only
- oversized cute eyes
- blue-skin shorthand
- glossy amphibian texture
- fantasy horns
- decorative bioluminescence
- mascot proportions
- fixed smile
- expressionless “wise alien” serenity
- anatomy that prevents believable eye-lines or speech

## Performance stress test

The approved face must support without redesign:

- scientific concentration
- confident misinterpretation of the umbrella
- embarrassment after being corrected
- small shared laugh
- recognition of Golden Record music
- concern when the local signal activates

## Approval freeze

Record:

- skull and facial proportions
- primary eye geometry
- secondary membrane behaviour
- ear geometry and permitted movement range
- skin material response
- mouth/speech anatomy
- neck-to-costume transition
- approved facial asymmetries

**File target:** `LBOE_PROOF_REF03_SCIENTIST-PORTRAIT_v##_APPROVED.png`

# 8. REF02 — BOY FULL BODY + WARDROBE

**Release priority:** third  
**Input required:** APPROVED REF01.

## Master prompt

**REF02 MASTER**  
Use approved REF01 face exactly. Production character turnaround for a capable human child living on reclaimed future Earth: front, side, rear three-quarter and practical walking pose on a neutral physically lit reference field. Compact child proportions, no heroic stance. Repaired weatherproof outer shell built from more than one plausible material, soft older domestic mid-layer, practical trousers with functional knee and pocket repairs, maintained footwear suitable for wet stone, shallow water and climbing, recognisable cross-body field bag/strap silhouette. Every repair has a practical reason; nothing decorative or tactical. Premium episodic animation with photographic material credibility, clear readable hands and footwear, neutral posture, restrained palette compatible with wet green and slate-blue Earth environments.

## Wardrobe questions every candidate must answer

- What is waterproof?
- What provides warmth?
- What has been outgrown or extended?
- Which repairs were done recently versus long ago?
- Where are tools carried?
- Can the silhouette be read from behind at 20 metres?
- Can the costume get wet without losing visual identity?

## Reject

- survival cosplay
- tactical vest
- pristine designer clothes
- decorative patch overload
- adult body proportions
- oversized backpack obscuring silhouette
- colour accents so strong they dominate the world

## Approval freeze

Record front/side/back:

- body proportions relative to head
- shoulder width
- standing posture
- stride baseline
- exact garment layers
- repair map
- field-bag dimensions and strap route
- shoe/boot geometry

# 9. REF04 — SCIENTIST FULL BODY + FIELD RECORDER

**Release priority:** fourth  
**Input required:** APPROVED REF03.

## Master prompt

**REF04 MASTER**  
Use approved REF03 face and species anatomy exactly. Production turnaround of the young alien scientist: front, profile, rear three-quarter and light walking stance, plus matched field-recorder object states. Lighter asymmetrical silhouette than command or security roles, layered repairable field garments, accessible scientific equipment, no armour. Posture attentive and slightly forward-balanced, able to lower naturally toward a human child's eye line. Four long fingers and one stabilising inner digit only if inherited from the approved anatomy test. Field recorder is palm-sized, tactile and layered, visibly handled and repaired, opens through physical articulated layers, information moves through embedded material channels, no floating screen. Show recorder closed, active with restrained amber trace, and safely offered toward a human hand. Premium episodic animation with photographic credibility and practical object scale.

## Recorder consistency test

The recorder fails if:

- its scale changes between alien and human hand
- it floats
- it produces generic holographic UI
- grip is anatomically impossible
- amber light becomes magical glow
- it looks like a smartphone, weapon or jewellery

## Approval freeze

Record:

- relative body proportions
- hand anatomy
- garment silhouette
- tool mounting positions
- recorder dimensions relative to palm
- recorder closed/open geometry
- active amber-path geometry

**File target:** `LBOE_PROOF_REF04_SCIENTIST-FULLBODY-RECORDER_v##_APPROVED.png`

# 10. REF05 — SECURITY OFFICER + PROTECTIVE TOOL

**Release priority:** fifth  
**Input required:** APPROVED REF03 and REF04 cultural grammar.

## Master prompt

**REF05 MASTER**  
Production silhouette and material reference for the alien expedition security officer, from the exact same civilisation as approved young scientist REF03/04. Broadest crew silhouette because of protective equipment and garment structure rather than monstrous anatomy. Planted, trained stance; calm neutral state must feel safe, tension state must feel formidable without changing costume. Layered repairable mineral-fibre clothing, compact equipment close to body, no military medals, no conventional gun. Primary protective tool is defensive and spatial: tactile layered device that maps structural risk, anchors unstable material and can create a brief physical barrier or restraint without injury. Tool shares the field recorder's material and interface grammar but has a distinct silhouette and heavier functional construction. Photographic material credibility, restrained colour, no tactical-science-fiction cliché.

## Dual-read test

Generate two matched poses with identical costume:

1. **Neutral:** relaxed hands, non-threatening weight distribution.
2. **Protective alert:** lateral weight shift, tool ready but not aimed as a weapon.

If the design needs a weapon pose to feel competent, reject it.

## Approval freeze

Record:

- silhouette width and height relationship
- protective garment/equipment zones
- hand and stance behaviour
- tool dimensions and carry position
- neutral versus alert posture
- shared civilisation materials inherited from REF04

**File target:** `LBOE_PROOF_REF05_SECURITY_v##_APPROVED.png`

# 11. Identity Gate scorecard

Every candidate is scored 1–5 against the same dimensions.

| Dimension | 1 | 3 | 5 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Canon safety | contradicts/open detail silently fixed | usable with caveats | fully safe and clearly provisional where needed |
| Emotional readability | generic / unreadable | one clear state | supports required micro-expressions |
| Continuity robustness | changes across views | mostly stable | stable across face/body/profile/action |
| Originality | cliché | competent | distinctive without novelty-for-novelty |
| Physical credibility | synthetic/impossible | plausible | tactile, weighted, lens/material coherent |
| Silhouette | unclear | recognisable | instantly readable at distance |
| Production repeatability | prompt-dependent | reproducible with effort | easily inherited by downstream shots |
| World/culture fit | disconnected | broadly compatible | unmistakably belongs to this project |

## Approval threshold

A candidate cannot be APPROVED if any dimension scores below **4**.

Target total: **34/40 or higher**.

A visually beautiful candidate that scores poorly on repeatability is rejected.

# 12. Cross-reference stress test

- boy from REF01/02
- scientist from REF03/04
- security officer from REF05
- neutral physical lighting
- simple standing interaction

This frame is not a hero image. It tests:

- relative stylistic fidelity
- material consistency
- whether all three characters appear to inhabit the same rendered world
- whether alien characters read as one civilisation
- whether the boy remains visually central without costume tricks

If this test exposes proportion problems, do not fix them by cheating the storyboard. Return to the relevant REF.
