# Greenlight Image Director

## Purpose
Create a coherent, full-bleed cinematic image system for premium series development proposals and PowerPoint decks. The skill works one approved image at a time, preserving canon, character identity, world rules and visual continuity across the entire image set.

## Required sources
Before creating any brief, read the latest versions of:
- `projects/<project>/CANON.md`
- `projects/<project>/characters/CORE_CHARACTERS.md`
- `projects/<project>/ART-DIRECTION-SHOT-BIBLE.md`
- `projects/<project>/story/SEASON_ONE_MAP.md`
- the approved image manifest for the project
- all previously approved images and review notes

## Non-negotiable principles
- Story first; technology second.
- Every image must communicate one clear dramatic idea.
- Every image must look as though it belongs to the same production.
- Character faces, ages, silhouettes, costume and scale must not drift.
- Full-bleed PowerPoint output is 16:9 landscape, minimum 3840 × 2160.
- Keep safe negative space for optional slide typography, but never place text inside generated art.
- Photoreal cinematic realism; no cartoon, anime, game-art or glossy generic CGI.
- Naturalistic Irish weather, tactile materials and believable light.
- Wonder before spectacle.

## Visual families
1. Character portraits — intimate, emotionally legible, 50–85mm lens language.
2. Epic environments — large-scale reclaimed Earth, 21–35mm lens language.
3. Story moments — decisive emotional or narrative beats.
4. World details — props, technology, Golden Record, Chorus and Haven details.
5. Mood plates — atmospheric section openers and transitions.
6. Concept graphics — maps, timelines and diagrams created separately from cinematic art.

## Output packet for every image

### Metadata
- Project
- Image number
- Page/slide destination
- Visual family
- Status: Brief / Generated / Review / Approved / Rejected
- Canon version read
- Previous approved references used

### Dramatic purpose
State the single idea the frame must communicate and why the reader needs this image.

### Composition
Describe framing, subject placement, visual hierarchy, negative space and foreground/midground/background structure.

### Character continuity
For every visible principal character specify age, face, silhouette, wardrobe, props, posture, expression and relationship to the other subjects.

### Environment continuity
Specify exact location, architecture, ecology, weather, season, time of day, materials and recurring identifiers.

### Camera and lens
Define camera height, lens family, field of view, depth of field and whether the frame feels intimate, observational or epic.

### Lighting and colour
Define key light, ambient conditions, contrast, haze and approved palette. Avoid unexplained neon, synthetic bloom and heavy teal/orange grading.

### Full generation brief
Write one clear, model-ready visual brief containing the scene, emotion, composition, continuity, camera, lighting, colour and realism requirements.

### Negative constraints
Always include: no text, no logos, no watermark, no extra limbs, no duplicate subjects, no malformed hands, no plastic skin, no generic flying saucer design, no fantasy armour, no neon interface clutter, no cartoon styling, no concept-art brushwork.

### PowerPoint crop check
Confirm that the image works full bleed at 16:9 and that essential faces and story information remain inside the safe centre area.

### Review score
Score out of 10:
- Storytelling
- Composition
- Cinematic realism
- Character continuity
- World continuity
- Lighting and colour
- PowerPoint suitability

Any category below 8 requires revision. Character or canon continuity below 9 requires rejection.

## Workflow
1. Read canon and visual sources.
2. Select the next incomplete image in the manifest.
3. Create the full image packet.
4. Generate one image.
5. Review against the packet and previous approved images.
6. Approve, revise or reject.
7. Record the decision and file path.
8. Move to the next image only after the current image is approved or explicitly deferred.

## File naming
`LBOE_PPT_001_cover-hero_v01.png`

Use:
- project code
- destination
- three-digit image number
- concise subject slug
- version number

## The Last Boy on Earth project look
- Reclaimed future Dublin, beautiful rather than ruined.
- Moss green, slate blue, wet stone, warm amber and aged bronze.
- Soft Irish overcast, mist, dawn and restrained golden-hour light.
- Tavi: childlike Vael explorer, blue-grey skin, large dark expressive eyes, organic cranial silhouette, simple desaturated natural-fibre clothing, no armour or implants.
- Eli: human boy, approximately 12–13, lean but credible, messy brown hair, weathered practical layers, repaired backpack, observant rather than action-hero posture.
- Vael craft and technology: organic, humane, restrained, tactile and non-militaristic.
- Golden Record: treated as emotionally sacred, warm bronze, minimal composition.

## Approval rule
If a new image cannot sit beside the approved references and convincingly appear to be a frame from the same film, reject it.
